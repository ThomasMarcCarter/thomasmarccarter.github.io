## Overview

A top-down twin-stick shooter where you fight through floors of zombie-filled rooms. You move with one stick and aim with the other, and your torch only lights what's in front of you. Each floor ends in a boss room, and clearing it opens the way down to the next one.

## What I built

- Twin-stick movement and aiming, with a main gun and a sidearm to swap between
- An inventory with a backpack grid, weapon attachments such as laser sights, drum mags and match barrels, and quick-use slots for medkits, grenades, molotovs and adrenaline
- A tactical map that tracks which rooms are cleared, which still have hostiles and where the treasure and boss are
- A HUD styled like a military display, with health, ammo, quick-use items and a live minimap
